This list of nearly 1,800 semantic domains was developed by Ron Moe, a linguist with SIL
Global, as a tool for collecting the words of a language. The pages below explain what a
semantic domain is, where this particular list came from, what people use it for, and how
it relates to two older classification systems that many domains cross-reference.

To browse the domains themselves, start from [Browse Domains](/v4/). To download the data
or read the sources behind the list, see [Data & Bibliography](/bibliography/).
