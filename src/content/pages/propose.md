The Semantic Domains list is maintained in the open, and it improves when the people who
use it in the field say what is missing or unclear. You do not need an account anywhere,
and you do not need to know how the data is stored.

Nothing sent from this page is published directly. A maintainer reviews every change,
because a domain number is permanent: dictionaries all over the world reference these
numbers in data this project cannot migrate.

Changes are made to **v5**, the draft under review. Version 4 is released and its
structure is locked; its text can only be corrected under a stricter policy.
